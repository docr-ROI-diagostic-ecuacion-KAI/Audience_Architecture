03 · Audience Architecture Map

Translate buyer-persona intelligence into targetable signals

Search intent and professional attributes are not the same thing. The map makes the translation explicit instead of treating “the buyer persona” as one universal audience.

1 · Business goalWhat economic outcome must be produced?
2 · Buyer personaWho has the problem, need, role or intent?
3 · Targetable signalQuery, job role, company, geography, first-party seed.
4 · Platform activationGoogle Search keyword or LinkedIn audience definition.
5 · EvidenceUTM, conversion, revenue / qualified lead and cost.

Google Search audience = expressed intent

Audience is primarily constructed from search language. The 4P keyword taxonomy becomes campaign / ad-group architecture and a paid-vs-organic decision layer.

LinkedIn audience = professional context

Audience is built from location, company, job experience and first-party / matched-audience signals. It should not be inferred from search keywords alone.

METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how audience evidence becomes an investable media decision

The laboratory turns business objectives, buyer-persona signals, search language and first-party evidence into a measurable activation plan for Google Search, LinkedIn and the SEO handoff.

D
Data

Strategic goals, paid-media budget, Buyer Persona, 4P keywords, CRM attribution and optional SPO customer evidence.

I
Information

The data is structured as objectives, audience signals, keyword intent, professional attributes, channel evidence and measurable constraints.

I
Intelligence

The lab translates signals into Google Search and LinkedIn activation logic, then models spend, clicks, conversions, leads and CPA/CPL scenarios.

I
Insights

The user can see where budget should be tested, which keywords deserve paid activation, what should move to SEO and where the target remains unsupported.

P
Personalization Actions

The output activates campaign structure, audience definitions, first-party seeds or exclusions, UTM governance, budget allocation and the organic-content backlog.
