DOC ROI · ROI INTELLIGENCE · ACQUISITION GOVERNANCE

From marketing investment to Customer ROI

Connect paid exposure, qualified demand, first purchases, customer-level attribution and the 18-month revenue path. Compare individual returns with your cost of capital, not just clicks or leads.

ROI = (Net revenue − Marketing OPEX) / Marketing OPEX
01 · Plan the economic target

Set 18-month volume, commercial price, tax, annual WACC and the costs included in Marketing OPEX.

02 · Observe the funnel

Media House reports impressions / CPM / CTR; MQL reports leads / CPL; SQL and Sales report qualified sales / CPA and revenue.

03 · Attribute customers

Join customer_key across RFM and orders. Attribute first purchases only with an explicit campaign / order mapping.

01 · ROI assumptions and the budget boundary

Do not double-count media costs: CPM, CPL and CPA are ratios calculated from paid spend, not additional expenses. Enter content, merchandising and legal only when not already included in another cost line. Gross consumer price is converted to net-of-VAT revenue for ROI. This is attributable marketing ROI, not company operating profit (product cost, COO, tax and other non-marketing costs are outside this numerator).

02 · Acquisition economics and reporting ownership

Import your RFM and attribution evidence or load the educational example.

Responsible evidence: Media Content House = impressions / CPM / CTR / spend; MQL = eligible leads / CPL; SQL = accepted opportunities; Sales = paid/verified orders, net revenue and CPA. All values above are refreshed from the form and exports.

03 · Customer ROI against annual WACC

Blue: customer-level annual-equivalent ROI. Red: annual WACC hurdle. Pale red: below-hurdle area. First purchases require an acquisition mapping; historical RFM value alone is insufficient.
Illustrative smoothed ROI density (Gaussian kernel); not proof that outcomes follow a normal distribution. Only customers with attributable revenue and cost are plotted.
