METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE PLAN

DIIIP explains how marketing evidence becomes an investable Audience → ROI decision

The plan turns customer language, strategic direction, budget, product evidence, commercial actions and operational readiness into a measurable activation plan for Google Search, LinkedIn and the SEO handoff.

D
Data

Buyer Persona / 4P, SWOT, Goals & Budget, Product & Experience, Commercial Actions and Stock / ERP readiness.

I
Information

The data is structured as objectives, audience signals, keyword intent, professional attributes, channel evidence and measurable constraints.

I
Intelligence

The lab translates signals into Google Search and LinkedIn activation logic, then models spend, clicks, conversions, leads and CPA/CPL scenarios.

I
Insights

The user can see where budget should be tested, which keywords deserve paid activation, what should move to SEO and where the target remains unsupported.

P
Personalization Actions

The output activates campaign structure, audience definitions, first-party seeds or exclusions, UTM governance, budget allocation and the organic-content backlog.
